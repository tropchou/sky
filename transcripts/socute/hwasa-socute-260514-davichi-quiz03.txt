ANSWER KEY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q1. A — 휩쓸리다 = to be swept away (by a current, crowd, mood, etc.). Hwasa used it about getting caught up in an atmosphere.

Q2. B — 인연 = a fateful/karmic connection between people, deeper than a casual encounter.

Q3. B — 숙명 specifically means inescapable destiny/fate, stronger than 운명. Hwasa framed loneliness as her 숙명.

Q4. B — 히트곡 = hit song (히트 from English "hit" + 곡 "song/piece").

Q5. C — 희석하다 = to dilute. 섞다 = mix; 녹이다 = melt/dissolve; 풀다 = loosen/solve.

Q6. B — 사투리 = regional dialect/accent. Hwasa was told her Jeonju accent doesn't show at all.

Q7. C — 고향 = hometown. 출신 means "originally from" but doesn't fit grammatically here as a standalone subject.

Q8. B — 답답하다 covers both physical stuffiness and emotional frustration/feeling stifled.

Q9. C — 스타성 (literally "star-ness") = the inherent quality that makes someone a star.

Q10. B — 원동력 = driving force / motive power. Often used for emotional or motivational energy.

Q11. B — 조롱하다 = to mock or ridicule someone, typically with cruelty or contempt.

Q12. B — 미련 specifically means lingering attachment that prevents one from letting go — distinct from 후회 (regret about a past action) or 아쉬움 (mild regret/wistfulness).

Q13. B — 얼짱 (얼굴 + 짱 "best") was a 2000s-era slang term for someone with an exceptionally good-looking face, often referencing internet-famous beauties.

Q14. C — 뿌듯하다 = the warm feeling of being proud/fulfilled by an accomplishment.

Q15. B — 꾸준하다 = to be steady/consistent over time. Distinct from "hardworking" (열심히) or "patient" (참을성 있다).

Q16. C — 얽매이다 = to be entangled/bound. Hwasa said she dislikes feeling tied down by routines.

Q17. B — 입가심 (literally "mouth-rinse") = a small bite or drink to finish off a meal or cleanse the palate.

Q18. A — 평온하다 = serene/calm/tranquil. Kang Min-kyung noted Hwasa was speaking in a 평온한 tone.

Q19. C — 호기심 = curiosity (literally "good-feeling-heart"). 관심 = interest/attention; 흥미 = interest in a topic; 욕심 = greed.

Q20. C — 치유 = healing, often emotional or spiritual. Hwasa said she feels healed by countryside visits.

Q21. B — 데뷔하다 = to debut (from French "début"). Hwasa mentioned she debuted in her senior year of high school.

Q22. C — 수도권 = the broader capital metropolitan region (Seoul + Gyeonggi + Incheon). 수도 alone just means "capital."

Q23. B — 시골 = campagne (countryside/rural area).

Q24. B — 엔터인 is industry slang (from "entertainer + 인 person") for someone working in entertainment as a performer.

Q25. B — 자취생 = someone (usually a student or young single person) living on their own and cooking/keeping house for themselves.

Q26. B — 즐기다 = to enjoy / take pleasure in something.

Q27. C — 외로움 = solitude/loneliness (the feeling). Distinct from 슬픔 (sadness) or 우울 (depression).

Q28. C — 봉골레 파스타 = vongole (clam) pasta. The dish Hwasa was making featured 조개 (clams).

Q29. B — 부럽다 → 부러웠어요 (past tense polite). She was envious of Western/foreign drinking-and-music-culture norms.

Q30. B — This best illustrates that she adapts her drinking style to her physical tolerance — she enjoys alcohol but in a controlled way (small throat = can't gulp; dilution = manages strength).
