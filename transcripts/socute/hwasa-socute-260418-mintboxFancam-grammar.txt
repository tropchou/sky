=== [02:17] 이럴 줄 알았어 ===

--- word ---
term: 이럴
root: 이렇다 (to be like this)
breakdown: 이렇 + ㄹ → 이럴
pos: adjective (prospective modifier form)
definition: (that it) would be like this
notes: 이렇다 belongs to the ㅎ-irregular adjective family: 이렇다, 그렇다, 저렇다, 어떻다. When followed by -(으)ㄹ, the ㅎ drops and ㄹ attaches directly: 이렇 → 이러 + ㄹ → 이럴. This is a key irregular conjugation pattern. Collocations: 이렇게 하다 (do it like this), 이런 사람 (this kind of person), 이럴 때 (at times like this). The 이- series refers to something close to the speaker or the current situation: 이렇다 (like this) vs. 그렇다 (like that — near listener or known) vs. 저렇다 (like that — far from both).

--- word ---
term: 줄
root: 줄 (way; fact; method)
breakdown: 줄
pos: bound noun (의존명사)
definition: the fact (that); the expectation (that)
notes: 줄 is a dependent/bound noun (의존명사) that cannot stand alone — it must be preceded by a modifier clause. It has two main uses: (1) V-(으)ㄹ 줄 알다/모르다 = to know/not know how to do something (ability), e.g., 수영할 줄 알아 (I can swim); (2) V-(으)ㄹ 줄 알다 = to expect/think that something would happen, e.g., 이럴 줄 알았어 (I knew it would be like this). Context distinguishes the two meanings. Here it's meaning (2): expectation. Collocations: 그럴 줄 알았어 (I knew it / I expected that), 올 줄 몰랐어 (I didn't know you'd come).

--- word ---
term: 알았어
root: 알다 (to know)
breakdown: 알 + 았 + 어
pos: verb (past tense, casual)
definition: knew; expected
notes: 알다 is an ㄹ-irregular verb — the ㄹ drops before ㄴ, ㅂ, ㅅ (e.g., 아는, 압니다, 아세요) but is retained before vowels (알아, 알았어). Past tense 알았어 here means "knew" or "expected" rather than "understood" — the meaning depends on the 줄 construction. Collocations: 알았어 (got it / understood — as a response), 알고 있어 (I already know), 알아보다 (to look into / investigate). Confusable: 알다 (to know a fact) vs. 알아보다 (to recognize / to find out).

--- grammar ---
pattern: V-(으)ㄹ 줄 알다 (expectation)
meaning: 'To expect/think that something would happen.' Past tense 알았어 confirms the expectation was correct: "I knew it would be like this." Distinguish from the ability meaning (할 줄 알다 = to know how to do). Context is key — if the subject of the -(으)ㄹ clause is a situation or third party, it's usually expectation; if it's the speaker's own skill, it's ability.
example: 이럴 줄 알았어
level: intermediate

--- grammar ---
pattern: ㅎ-irregular adjective conjugation
meaning: Adjectives ending in ㅎ (이렇다, 그렇다, 저렇다, 어떻다, 빨갛다, 파랗다) drop the ㅎ before vowel-initial endings. Before -(으)ㄹ: 이렇 → 이러 + ㄹ → 이럴. Before -아/어: 이렇 → 이래. This is one of the trickier irregular patterns to master.
example: 이럴
level: intermediate

--- grammar ---
pattern: V-(으)ㄹ N (prospective modifier)
meaning: Modifier form expressing future/prospective action or state. -(으)ㄹ before a noun means 'that will / would [verb].' Here 이럴 modifies the bound noun 줄: "the fact that it would be like this." Other examples: 먹을 것 (something to eat), 갈 곳 (a place to go).
example: 이럴 줄
level: intermediate
