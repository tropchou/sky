ANSWER KEY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q1. B — 막창 is the last/end portion of beef intestine, distinct from 곱창 (small intestine).

Q2. C — 무대 means "stage" → scène in French.

Q3. B — 본명 means one's real/given name. Hwasa (stage name) prefers being called by her real name, Ahn Hyejin.

Q4. C — 사교성 = sociability, the trait of being socially outgoing.

Q5. C — 컴백 is the standard K-pop industry loanword for "comeback." 복귀 means "return" generally but isn't the music-industry term.

Q6. B — Kang Min-kyung brought wine (와인) as a gift.

Q7. C — 취향 = goût/préférence (taste/preference).

Q8. B — 감동 specifically means being emotionally moved or touched, not just surprised or excited.

Q9. C — 화병 = vase. Note that 화분 means flower pot (for live plants), not a vase for cut flowers.

Q10. B — 회사원 = office worker / company employee. 직원 means "staff/employee" more generally.

Q11. C — 선배님 is a respectful term for someone senior in your profession or school.

Q12. B — 화상 = burn (injury); 화상을 입다 = to suffer a burn. (Note: 화상 can also mean "video image," but in this transcript context it refers to a burn.)

Q13. C — 먹방 (먹는 방송) literally means "eating broadcast" — the well-known mukbang genre.

Q14. B — 비즈니스 스케쥴 was the exact phrase used to suggest coordinating their professional schedules.

Q15. C — 대중성 refers to mass/popular appeal — being accessible to the general public.

Q16. B — 복장 = tenue/habillement (outfit/attire).

Q17. B — 세지다 means to become stronger/more intense. So her taste is becoming more intense/bold, not milder.

Q18. C — 성격 = personality/character. 인격 means "character" in a more moral/dignity sense; 인상 = impression; 기분 = mood.

Q19. C — 공연 = spectacle/performance.

Q20. B — 막창 literally contains 막 ("last/final") + 창 (from 창자, intestine), referring to the end portion (the abomasum in cattle).

Q21. C — 본명 = real/given name, as opposed to a stage name (예명) or nickname (별명).

Q22. B — 무대 (stage) was the subject of the compliment "무대가 너무 근사했고."

Q23. B — 만나다 = to meet (someone, by arrangement). 마주치다 means to bump into accidentally.

Q24. C — 사교성 = sociabilité.

Q25. B — 술 (alcohol). She wanted to buy nice alcohol as a gift.

Q26. B — 귀엽다 = to be cute/adorable. 예쁘다 covers "beautiful/pretty."

Q27. A — 화병 = vase. Bouteille = 병, pot de fleurs = 화분.

Q28. C — 성격 (personality) was the basis of comparison — others told Kang Min-kyung that she and Hwasa had similar personalities.

Q29. B — 화병 is the word used in the transcript. 꽃병 is also valid Korean for "vase" but 화병 was the term Hwasa used.

Q30. C — 감동 (being moved/touched). Hwasa expressed how emotionally moved she was.
