ANSWER KEY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q1. B — 편식하다 = to eat selectively / be picky about food (편 "biased" + 식 "eating").

Q2. C — 해산물 = fruits de mer (seafood). 海産物 literally "sea-produced goods."

Q3. B — 주량 = drinking capacity/alcohol tolerance. 음주 means "drinking (alcohol)" as an activity, not capacity.

Q4. C — 고수 in food context = cilantro/coriander. (Note: 고수 has a homonym meaning "master/expert," but here it's the herb.)

Q5. B — 설레다 specifically captures the fluttery, anticipatory excitement. 떨리다 = to tremble (often nervously); 두근거리다 = heart pounding (similar but more physical).

Q6. B — 낯설다 = to be unfamiliar/strange (literally "face-unfamiliar"). Distinct from 낯가리다 (shy with strangers).

Q7. C — 일정 was the word used. While 스케줄 also means schedule, 일정 was the specific term in the transcript.

Q8. B — 멘탈 나가다 (literally "mental goes out") = to mentally break down / lose composure. Modern slang.

Q9. C — 청양고추 is a specific named variety of Korean chili, known for being especially spicy.

Q10. C — 임원 = corporate executive / board member. Kang Min-kyung joked that Hwasa's outfit made her look like an executive.

Q11. B — 낯가리다 (literally "to discriminate by face") = to be shy or reserved with people one doesn't know well.

Q12. B — 슬랙스 (slacks/dress pants) was the base of the outfit being described.

Q13. B — 주관하다 = to take charge of / organize / oversee an event or project.

Q14. C — 향신료 = épice/condiment (spice/seasoning).

Q15. B — 탄산 = carbonation. Hwasa was asked if she likes carbonated alcoholic drinks.

Q16. C — 발라드 (ballad). Hwasa identifies as a ballad singer in this context, contrasting with Beyoncé's genre but admiring her anyway.

Q17. C — 기가 막히다 (literally "energy/breath is blocked") idiomatically expresses being stunned — usually by something amazing, though it can also express disbelief negatively. In context, it was positive.

Q18. B — 로망 (from French "roman") in Korean usage means an idealized dream or fantasy — something you romanticize.

Q19. C — 로퍼 (loafer) is the direct loanword. 구두 = dress shoes generally; 운동화 = sneakers.

Q20. B — 쉬고 싶어요 = "I want to rest." 쉬다 = to rest.

Q21. B — 의욕 = motivation/drive/will to do something. Hwasa mentioned watching Beyoncé concerts when her motivation drops.

Q22. C — 심쿵하다 (slang, 심장 + 쿵 onomatopoeia) = heart-thump / heart-skip-a-beat moment.

Q23. B — 회 = raw fish/sashimi. 생선 = fish (as food generally, cooked or raw); 초밥 = sushi.

Q24. B — 유행어 = trendy/buzz word. 신조어 = newly coined word (related but distinct); 줄임말 = abbreviation.

Q25. B — 고생하다 = to suffer hardship / work hard. Often used as 고생하셨어요 to acknowledge someone's effort.

Q26. B — 존경하다 = to respect / admire (in a looking-up-to sense).

Q27. C — 목구멍 = throat. Hwasa said her throat is small, so she can't gulp large amounts at once.

Q28. B — 어색하다 = to feel awkward / uncomfortable in a situation.

Q29. B — Both Hwasa and Kang Min-kyung agreed they drink more (많이 마신다) when feeling shy or awkward.

Q30. C — 샴페인 = champagne. Hwasa said it's her favorite.
