# Answer Key

**Q1 — B.** 처절하다 means to be bleak/desperate/heartrending. The host used it to describe the lyrics of "Good Goodbye": "가사가 좀 처절하고 슬픈 느낌도 있는 노래니까."

**Q2 — C.** Hwasa said the worst kind of breakup is one done without emotion (감정 없이 하는 이별). She then immediately caught herself, saying "아 근데 또 이렇게 단언하면 안 되는데" — she shouldn't make such definitive statements.

**Q3 — B.** 공감 means empathy/sympathy/relating to. Hanhae used it when he said "네 공감이 됩니다" — yes, I relate to that.

**Q4 — B.** 물망초 is the forget-me-not flower, which translates to *myosotis / ne-m'oublie-pas* in French.

**Q5 — C.** "고맙다란 말도 미안해라는 말도 너무 너무 **하찮아**" — saying thank you and sorry both feel too trivial/insignificant.

**Q6 — B.** The flower language of 물망초 is "나를 잊지 마세요" — "Please don't forget me."

**Q7 — C.** 만족 means satisfaction/contentment. Hwasa said during the Guilty Pleasure era "만족을 못 느껴서" — she couldn't feel satisfied.

**Q8 — C.** 확신 translates to *certitude / conviction*. Hwasa said "이제는 조금 확신이 서요" — now I feel a bit more certain/confident.

**Q9 — B.** 굿즈 (from the English "goods") means goods/merch/merchandise. It refers to official fan merchandise.

**Q10 — C.** 덤덤해지네 (I'm Becoming Calm), from Mamamoo's 2018 album Yellow Flower, was Hwasa's first song where she fully handled both lyrics and composition: "공식적으로 작사 작곡을 완전히 딱 한 게 이 곡이 처음이에요."

**Q11 — B.** "걸어온 길이 **험했을** 뿐이야" — "It's just that the road I walked was rough." This was a lyric from 로그송 (Log Song) that Hwasa discussed as reflecting her real life.

**Q12 — C.** 후회하다 translates to *regretter* in French.

**Q13 — C.** 미련 means lingering attachment / reluctance to let go. Hwasa used it when discussing the forget-me-not's flower language: "어떻게 보면 좀 미련할 수도 있는 꽃말이잖아요."

**Q14 — C.** The foreign fan asked if Hwasa had thought about making an official lightstick (공식 화사 응원봉), saying fans — especially 청이들 — really want one.

**Q15 — B.** 사색 translates to *méditation / contemplation / réflexion profonde*. Hanhae asked Hwasa "평소에 좀 사색을 해요?" and she replied she does it constantly, to the point where it's exhausting.

**Q16 — B.** Hwasa said when her mind gets too noisy (속이 시끄러워), she escapes to Jeju Island alone, sometimes staying for a month. She compared it to running away to somewhere with no sound.

**Q17 — C.** 극복하다 means to overcome/get through. Hwasa said "힘든 일들 너무 많지만 극복할 수 있을 것 같아요" — there are many difficult things, but she thinks she can overcome them.

**Q18 — B.** 응원 translates to *soutien / encouragement*. Hwasa described the core emotion of "Good Goodbye" as "사랑이고 응원" — love and support.

**Q19 — B.** Hwasa's father told her: "한 번 웃으면 걱정이 두 개가 사라진다" — if you smile once, two worries disappear. She called it cheesy (유치하게) but deeply touching, and she put it into the final lyrics of 덤덤해지네.

**Q20 — C.** 단단하다 means to be solid/tough/hardened (emotionally). Hwasa said "내가 이렇게 단련이 됐고 단단해져 있는 모습인 것 같아요" — she has been forged and hardened through experience.

**Q21 — B.** Hwasa said the feeling behind "Good Goodbye" (좋은 안녕) is "사랑이고 응원" — love and support. The lyric means: even if I regret it, I want you to laugh big. It's about wishing the other person well.

**Q22 — C.** 꽃말 translates to *langage des fleurs* in French.

**Q23 — C.** Hwasa chose 마리아 (Maria), saying she had been envisioning the song since her trainee days and that it captured everything she wanted to express. She also noted it's her most loved song.

**Q24 — C.** "힘든 일들 너무 많지만 **극복할** 수 있을 것 같아요" — there are many difficult things, but I think I can overcome them.

**Q25 — B.** 하찮다 means to be trivial/insignificant/worthless. Hwasa used it to describe how "thank you" and "sorry" felt too trivial to express her real emotions at the moment of parting.

**Q26 — C.** Hwasa said Guilty Pleasure represented the peak (절정) of her most painful times — she couldn't feel satisfied (만족을 못 느껴서), was flooded with dopamine, catharsis, and adrenaline from her profession, and nothing ordinary could impress her anymore.

**Q27 — B.** 헤어지다 translates to *se séparer / rompre* in French.

**Q28 — C.** The fan name is 멍청이 (idiot, after her solo song), shortened affectionately to 청이들. Hwasa noted that before being 멍청이들, they are also 무무들 (Moomoos, Mamamoo's fandom).

**Q29 — C.** Hwasa said she has always loved Latin music and has been sprinkling drops of it into songs like Maria's dance break, but she wants to do a fully, intensely Latin project someday — "농도 짙게 한번 그냥 라틴 라틴 하게."

**Q30 — B.** Hwasa said she loves everything fans say — except 헛소리 (nonsense/bullshit). She gave the example of fans saying things like "언니 어느 별에서 왔어요?" (Sister, what planet are you from?) — but then admitted that kind of 주접 (cheesy fawning) is fun too.
