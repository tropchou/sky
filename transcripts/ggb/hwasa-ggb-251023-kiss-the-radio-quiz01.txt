# Answer Key

**Q1 — B.** 소름 means goosebumps/chills. In the transcript, a fan comment describes how a good song gives you 소름과 전율 (goosebumps and shivers) from the very first notes.

**Q2 — C.** Hanhae explained that he and Hwasa were at the same entertainment company about 15 years ago, and he taught her and Moonbyul how to rap.

**Q3 — C.** 애정 means affection/fondness. 감동 means being moved/touched, 인연 means connection/bond, and 이별 means parting/breakup.

**Q4 — B.** 이별 translates to *séparation / rupture* in French. *Adieu / au revoir* would be a more general goodbye rather than the specific sense of a breakup or parting.

**Q5 — C.** The full sentence is: "좋은 곡은 반주부터 **소름**과 전율이 느껴지는데" — a good song gives you goosebumps and shivers from the accompaniment.

**Q6 — B.** Hwasa explained that in January she dropped a dumbbell on her toe and it fractured (골절), forcing her to delay the originally planned March release. She waited until October.

**Q7 — C.** 가사 means lyrics. 곡 means song/track, 노래 also means song (more general), and 신곡 means new song.

**Q8 — B.** 감동 translates to *émotion / être touché* in French — the feeling of being emotionally moved.

**Q9 — B.** 화양연화 refers to the most beautiful moment in life / the prime of youth. Hwasa used it to describe the emotional content of her song.

**Q10 — C.** "쉬운 **이별**은 없죠" — "There is no easy breakup."

**Q11 — B.** Hwasa said the song "5" (오) is the only song she wrote when she was truly, purely happy — happy for no reason at all.

**Q12 — B.** 섭외 means casting/recruiting/booking a guest. In the transcript, the host describes Hwasa's letter to Park Jungmin as a form of 섭외.

**Q13 — B.** 뮤지션 translates to *musicien(ne)* in French. While *artiste* and *chanteur* are related, the direct translation of 뮤지션 is musician.

**Q14 — C.** Hwasa explained that while the public sees her as strong and powerful, the image in "Good Goodbye" reflects her inner self — vulnerable and soft when in love. She said "누구나 사랑을 할 때 연약하는 모습들이 다 있다" and "그 정반대되는 이미지가 제 내면 모습이에요."

**Q15 — D.** 성숙하다 means to be mature. 강인하다 means to be strong/tough, 연약하다 means to be fragile, and 행복하다 means to be happy.

**Q16 — B.** Hwasa wrote Park Jungmin a handwritten letter that took her about 5 hours, which she delivered through a mutual acquaintance.

**Q17 — C.** 골절 translates to *fracture (osseuse)* in French.

**Q18 — B.** 컴백 means comeback — a return to the music scene with new material. A fan message in the transcript read "컴백 만세" (long live the comeback).

**Q19 — B.** "한층 깊어진 감성이 담긴 **신곡**" — "A new song with deeper emotions," from the host's introduction of Hwasa.

**Q20 — B.** Hwasa firmly stated she has no intention of acting professionally. She explained that she separates what she loves from what she's good at, and she will channel her love of acting exclusively into her own music videos.

**Q21 — B.** 화양연화 translates to *l'âge d'or / le plus beau moment de la vie* in French.

**Q22 — B.** 환영 means welcome. The host said "너무나 환영입니다" when greeting Hwasa.

**Q23 — B.** The host described the music video's texture as very vintage (빈티지), comparing it to an independent film (독립영화) and a foreign film (해외영화).

**Q24 — C.** 연약하다 means to be fragile/vulnerable. Hwasa used it when discussing how everyone has a vulnerable side when in love.

**Q25 — C.** 다이어트 translates to *régime (alimentaire)* in French.

**Q26 — B.** Hwasa said "Good Goodbye" was her first love-themed title track since 멍청이 (Idiot). She noted that her other titles had been more about self-confidence.

**Q27 — B.** "아티스트는 자기 **곡**을 사랑해야 된다고 생각합니다" — "I think artists should love their songs."

**Q28 — C.** 인연 means connection/fate/bond. It refers to a meaningful link between people, often with a sense of destiny. It's broader than just romantic relationships and doesn't carry the negative connotation of "unavoidable doom."

**Q29 — B.** Hwasa named *Betty Blue*, *Eat Pray Love* (먹고기도하고 사랑하라), and a Paul Thomas Anderson film (*One Battle After Another*) that she hadn't yet watched.

**Q30 — C.** 가사 translates to *paroles (d'une chanson)* in French.
