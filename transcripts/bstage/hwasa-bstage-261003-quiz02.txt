# Answer Key

**Q1 — C)** She has just realized or taken in new information ("I see!")
-구나 marks a new realization or reaction to information, so "그렇구나" means "I see." It is not a question or a suggestion.

**Q2 — A)** Picking her nose (and seducing)
She jokes that her nails are perfect for picking her nose, and then adds that they are good for seducing too.

**Q3 — C)** To seduce or tempt
유혹하다 means to seduce or tempt. 유혹하기도 좋아 = "(my nails) are good for seducing too."

**Q4 — D)** She prefers that the fan forgets her, so that she bears the pain
She says it is better if the fan forgets unni, and in the next line she says she will take the pain herself.

**Q5 — A)** Her will or determination to do it
With a first-person subject, -겠다 shows a firm intention: "I will take the pain."

**Q6 — D)** 이
팬들이 is the subject, the ones singing: "Fans singing at my wedding." 을, 으로 and 에서 do not mark the subject.

**Q7 — D)** Fans singing at her own wedding
She picks the version where fans sing at her wedding, not the one where she sings at a fan's wedding.

**Q8 — A)** "You know, it's obvious", reminding the listener of something shared
-잖아 presents something as obvious or already known: "That would be fun, you know."

**Q9 — D)** L'étoffe d'une prof principale
(Noun) 재질 means 'the makings / vibe of'. Here it is a homeroom-teacher vibe.

**Q10 — A)** Crying a lot, like Kiyoung
She imagines herself crying her eyes out like Kiyoung (울고 있고 = being in the state of crying).

**Q11 — A)** You'd get kicked out
쫓겨나다 is to be kicked out or driven away. 안 돼 = "No way", so you'd obviously get kicked out.

**Q12 — A)** 가
머리가 is the subject of 감당이 안 되다: "My hair is so unmanageable." The other particles do not fit as the subject.

**Q13 — C)** 니까
-(으)니까 gives the reason: "Since my nose is stuffed, pronunciation and such is really hard." 지만 would make a contrast, which does not fit.

**Q14 — B)** Because her nose is stuffed
She says 내 코가 막히니까, so the blocked nose is the reason pronunciation is hard.

**Q15 — B)** 을
단어들을 is the object of 줘: "Give me difficult words." 이 would make them the subject, which does not fit.

**Q16 — B)** Surprised or protesting repetition of what was just said ("what do you mean...?")
-다니 repeats someone's words with surprise or objection, so 안타깝다니 means "What do you mean, 'a pity'?"

**Q17 — A)** 안타깝다 is regret about a situation; 불쌍하다 is pity for a poor soul
안타깝다 expresses regret or frustration at a situation, while 불쌍하다 expresses pity toward someone.

**Q18 — A)** Vous exagérez !
너무하다 used as a reaction means "that's too much / you're going too far." It is a complaint, not a compliment.

**Q19 — B)** To kick out everyone acting or saying weird things
강퇴 is a kick or ban. She says all those acting or speaking strangely are kicked out.

**Q20 — D)** Why they like yukgaejang so much
왜 ... 너무 좋아해 asks "Why do you like yukgaejang that much?"

**Q21 — B)** 이랑
팬들이랑 means "with the fans": "What are you doing with the fans?" The other particles do not mean 'with'.

**Q22 — D)** Laisse-moi rire, n'importe quoi
웃기고 있어 is sarcastic: "You're being ridiculous." It does not mean she is happily laughing.

**Q23 — C)** 으러
-(으)러 가다 means go in order to do something: "The staff said they were going to eat dim sum." The other endings do not express purpose.

**Q24 — D)** "You could say / sort of", a hedge on how she phrases it
-달까 is a hedge. She sort of gave the card, so "I guess you could say I gave them my card."

**Q25 — B)** She gave them her card
She says she couldn't go, so she gave them her card.

**Q26 — C)** 에
에 marks the location with 있다: "The egg tarts are in the fridge right now." The other particles do not mark location, and 이 and 은 are wrong after the vowel ending 고.

**Q27 — A)** Hong Kong waffles
She says she ate a lot of Hong Kong waffles earlier. The egg tarts are still in the fridge.

**Q28 — C)** Correcting a misunderstanding: "I'm not saying that..."
-다는 게 아니야 denies what someone might assume she means: "I'm not saying I ate."

**Q29 — B)** 은 지
V-(으)ㄴ 지 + 오래되다 means "it has been a long time since...": "Wow, it's been a really long time since I've had Yeopddeok."

**Q30 — B)** It gives background or a reason, as an explanation
-거든 gives a reason or background that the listener doesn't know: "I watch a lot of stuff like that, you see."

**Q31 — D)** A resolution to herself: "I should / I must"
-어야지 here is a self-directed resolution: "I should make it like this and eat it."

**Q32 — C)** 저장하고
"하면서 맨날 저장하고 이러다 보니까" means "...and I always save them and so on." 삭제하다 (delete) would contradict the context.

**Q33 — A)** She constantly watches and saves cooking videos
She says she watches cooking videos all the time and saves them. That is why her algorithm shows only that kind of content.

**Q34 — B)** 알고리즘
"이제 알고리즘이 다 그런 것만 뜨더라고" means "Now my algorithm only shows things like that." 알고리즘 is the thing that makes content pop up.

**Q35 — B)** It can't be helped
어쩔 수 없다 means "there is nothing one can do", i.e. "it can't be helped."

**Q36 — D)** 이해해줘야
"아프니까 이해해줘야 돼" means "I'm sick, so you have to understand." 무시해줘야 would not make sense as a request.

**Q37 — C)** To crush or break into pieces
부수다 is to break or crush. She crushes the ramen noodles before adding rice.

**Q38 — A)** Emphasis: "I'm telling you", insisting on her point
-(ㄴ/는)단 말이야 stresses a statement: "I haven't taken my medicine yet, I'm telling you."

**Q39 — D)** With cup ramen
She says you make and eat Buldak risotto with cup ramen (컵라면으로), crushing the noodles and adding rice.

**Q40 — C)** 중독
"그리고 중독이 된다" means "And it becomes addictive." The other nouns make no sense here.

**Q41 — B)** They are going crazy (can't stand it)
미치다 means to go crazy. Here it means people are driven crazy by their craving.

**Q42 — C)** 경고
"They would give a warning in advance to people who hadn't tried it." 경고를 하다 is to warn. The other options don't fit.

**Q43 — D)** To get hooked or fixated on something
꽂히다 colloquially means getting fixated on something. She keeps eating what she gets hooked on.

**Q44 — C)** She has eaten it four times recently
She says she has just eaten jokbal four times lately, showing how hooked she is.

**Q45 — D)** Le besoin était revenu, comme une capacité de jeu rechargée, et je l'ai satisfait
쿨타임 (cooldown) is game slang. Here her craving came back like a recharged skill, and she satisfied it all at once.

**Q46 — A)** 더니
-았/었더니 means "I did X and then found Y": "So I put it on and it was fine." The other endings do not describe a past result.

**Q47 — A)** To apply or spread (a cream)
바르다 is to apply or spread something like cream on skin.

**Q48 — B)** 향기
"그러니까 평소에는 향기 나는 걸 좋아하는데" means "So normally I like scented things." 향기 나다 means to have a scent.

**Q49 — C)** J'insiste : j'en ai déjà dans mon frigo
Here -다고 insists on a statement the listener seems to have missed: "I already have some in my fridge, I'm telling you."

**Q50 — B)** Tu n'as toujours pas fini ta journée de travail ?
퇴근하다 is to leave work at the end of the day. She asks informally (반말) whether the person is still at work.
