# Answer Key

**Q1 — B)** To have a rich, earthy, savory flavor. 구수하다 is a distinctly Korean taste descriptor for the warm, deep savoriness found in roasted grains, doenjang, or nurungji. It's not sour (A) or bitter (D).

**Q2 — C)** Comfort / consolation. 위로 describes emotional solace — the feeling of being comforted. She was saying the food felt like receiving consolation.

**Q3 — A)** 머무르다. It means to stay, remain, or linger in a place or state. 꾸준히 is an adverb (consistently), 감사하다 means grateful, 충족시키다 means to satisfy.

**Q4 — B)** Être fade / insipide. 맹맹하다 describes food that lacks flavor — flat and bland. Not spicy (A) or bitter (C).

**Q5 — C)** 충족. "It feels like it satisfies/fulfills a desire" (욕구를 충족시키다).

**Q6 — C)** Something thick and creamy / dense in texture. 꾸덕하다 is a popular Korean texture word for rich, dense creaminess — think thick cream cheese or dense brownie. The opposite of watery (A) or airy (B).

**Q7 — C)** Nuts / tree nuts. 견과류 (堅果類) refers to nuts — almonds, walnuts, cashews, etc. Dried fruit (A) would be 건과일 or 말린 과일.

**Q8 — B)** Avoir sommeil / être somnolent(e). 졸리다 specifically means to be sleepy/drowsy, which is more precise than just being tired or fatigued (A).

**Q9 — C)** 꾸준히. It means consistently or steadily over time. 열심히 (B) means diligently/hard, which is about effort intensity rather than regularity.

**Q10 — C)** Business / trade (small-scale). 장사 typically refers to running a small business or shop. She was speculating about what she'd do if she weren't in the entertainment industry.

**Q11 — B)** Satisfaire / combler. 충족시키다 means to satisfy or fulfill a need/desire. Provoquer (A) means to provoke, which is the opposite effect.

**Q12 — B)** She was joking that her tendency to drop spoilers (스포) was like a contagious disease (전염병) spreading everywhere — she can't help herself.

**Q13 — C)** Pros and cons debate. 찬반 combines 찬 (찬성, for/agree) and 반 (반대, against/disagree), and 토론 means debate/discussion.

**Q14 — C)** 기부하다. It specifically means to donate or give to charity. 나눠주다 (A) means to share/distribute, 양보하다 (B) means to yield/concede.

**Q15 — B)** 초호화. "I'm going to release a super luxurious album" (초호화 앨범).

**Q16 — B)** Proceeds / profits / revenue. 수익금 (收益金) is the money earned/generated, not the costs of production (A) or specifically royalties (D).

**Q17 — C)** Donate it. She said 다 기부해 — donate it all.

**Q18 — B)** Professionnel(le) / spécialisé(e). 전문적 means professional or specialized. Amateur (A) would be the opposite.

**Q19 — C)** Planner type / organized type. 계획 means plan, and 형 means type/style. A 계획형 person likes to organize and plan ahead.

**Q20 — B)** 무계획형 — she identified as the spontaneous, unplanned type. 무 (無) negates 계획 (plan), so 무계획형 = non-planner type.

**Q21 — C)** Demande en mariage / proposition. 프로포즈 is a Konglish borrowing of "propose," used specifically for marriage proposals. Not a business proposition (B).

**Q22 — B)** Having bright, sparkling eyes. 초롱초롱하다 is a mimetic/descriptive word for eyes that look clear, bright, and twinkling.

**Q23 — B)** Yawning had the effect of making her eyes look bright and sparkly. The reflex tears from yawning (하품) made her eyes glisten, and she jokingly called it a 하품 효과 (yawn effect).

**Q24 — C)** 열심히 살다. 열심히 means diligently/hard, and 살다 means to live. Together: to live life with effort and dedication.

**Q25 — D)** 계획형. "A partner who's a planner type vs. spontaneous type" (계획형 대 무계획형).

**Q26 — C)** Desire / need / urge. 욕구 is a broad term for a desire or basic need, not limited to food appetite (D) or competitive ambition (A).

**Q27 — B)** 욕구 refers to a basic desire, need, or urge (often neutral), while 욕심 implies a more excessive wanting — greed, ambition, or a desire beyond what's necessary. They overlap but carry different weight.

**Q28 — A)** She was humbly downplaying her expertise in something outside her primary field, saying she's not a specialist or professional in that area.

**Q29 — C)** Faire un don / donner. 기부하다 means to donate or give charitably. Investir (A) means to invest, and emprunter (D) means to borrow — both unrelated.

**Q30 — B)** Even though the taste was flat/bland, it still satisfied a certain craving or need. She was making a nuanced food observation — something doesn't have to be intensely flavorful to be satisfying on a deeper level.
