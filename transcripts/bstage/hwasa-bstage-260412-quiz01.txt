# Answer Key

**Q1 — D)** 버렸다
"참지 못하고 오늘 와버렸다" = "I couldn't hold back, so I ended up coming on today." -아/어 버리다 in plain past form -았다 style ends the statement; the others are a question, a suggestion and a modifier.

**Q2 — D)** She ate duck, about one or two whole ducks
She says she ate one or two ducks. She does not claim to have drunk much.

**Q3 — D)** Because Hwasa was coming, he fetched dureup and mushrooms himself
The owner went himself because she was coming, and brought back dureup, mushrooms and other delicacies.

**Q4 — B)** 서
"마시는게 아니라 너무 많이 먹어서" = "it's that I ate too much." -어서 gives the reason; 서 attaches to 먹어. The others are not connectors.

**Q5 — B)** A possibility: you might tease me
-ㄹ 수도 있다 = "might / could possibly". It indicates a possibility, not ability or command.

**Q6 — B)** Censorship: covering her belly
It is the running gag: she needs to censor her belly before showing it.

**Q7 — D)** 치
"보여주면 안 되는데 그치?" = "I shouldn't be showing it, right?" 그치 seeks agreement.

**Q8 — D)** 컬러
"내 퍼스널 컬러야" = "it's my personal color," her joke about yellow lighting.

**Q9 — A)** Exclamatory emphasis on how much, recalling the past
The pattern recalls a past feeling emphatically: "how my happiness soared!"

**Q10 — C)** To be familiar
She jokes that the yellow lighting is very familiar to the viewers.

**Q11 — C)** One person ate two packs
She reports that one person ate two packs because it was so good.

**Q12 — B)** 냐고
"어디 거냐고?" = "You're asking where it's from?" -냐고 echoes a viewer's question.

**Q13 — C)** un côté injeolmi, un petit air d'injeolmi
느낌 = "feel, vibe": the tteok has an injeolmi-like feel.

**Q14 — D)** The tteok made her mouth very dry
After eating the tteok her mouth kept drying out.

**Q15 — A)** Being busy with one action, which causes a result
-느라 means one action takes up time/effort and causes another result: chewing used up the time.

**Q16 — B)** 씹
씹다 = to chew. "떡 씹느라" = because chewing the tteok.

**Q17 — A)** 던지
"어찌나 미안하던지" = "I felt so bad." 어찌나 ...던지 expresses strong recalled feeling.

**Q18 — C)** Pumpkin porridge
호박죽 is pumpkin porridge; warm, the tteok would taste like it.

**Q19 — D)** 23:39 (11:39 PM), so fans should go home
She tells fans to hurry home because it's already 11:39 at night.

**Q20 — B)** 이
"창호떡이 지금 품절이 됐어?" = "Changeok tteok is sold out now?" 이 marks the subject of 됐어; 떡 ends in a consonant.

**Q21 — C)** "even if / although": they didn't eat despite being told
-아/어도 means "even if/though", so people wouldn't eat even when told.

**Q22 — C)** 거든
"막 이랬었거든?" = "it was like that, you know?" -거든 adds background explanation.

**Q23 — A)** To feel refreshed and light
After finishing all her work she feels refreshed.

**Q24 — D)** People said it was not good, just tteok, when she urged them to eat it
Fans/people said it was bad or just tteok, while now Byul-i unnie says it's good.

**Q25 — A)** C'est ça, le problème. (ton familier)
-야 est une terminaison familière (반말) d'affirmation.

**Q26 — B)** 지
"너무 가고 싶지" = "I really want to go, you know." -지 seeks shared understanding.

**Q27 — D)** Thawing it
해동시키다 = to thaw; she thawed tteok every dawn.

**Q28 — A)** 으로
으로 shows the form/state in which she eats it: frozen tteok.

**Q29 — C)** 냉동
"근데 나는 냉동떡으로 먹고 있어" = "But I eat it frozen."

**Q30 — A)** Curious
궁금하다 = to be curious.

**Q31 — B)** 에
N에 빠지다 = to fall into/be hooked on. "사랑에 빠진 게 또 있어" = "I've fallen for something else too."

**Q32 — B)** Fried lotus-root chips
She says she has fallen for lotus-root chips (yeongeun-bugak).

**Q33 — C)** 겠어
"내가 먹고 싶은 거 다 먹겠어" = "I'm going to eat everything I want." -겠어 shows determination.

**Q34 — D)** Don't order lotus chips just anywhere; this brand is good
She recommends this particular place.

**Q35 — A)** Un goût parfait pour accompagner l'alcool
술안주 = amuse-gueule pour boire; 딱 좋다 = parfait.

**Q36 — A)** To be similar
A이랑 비슷하다 = similar to A.

**Q37 — C)** Creamy potato chips
She compares them to creamy potato chips.

**Q38 — A)** At an acquaintance's house
The acquaintance served them and they were very good.

**Q39 — A)** To take out
The acquaintance took the chips out and served them.

**Q40 — B)** About 2 hours
She says she slept about 2 hours (and then none before Inkigayo).

**Q41 — C)** With a negative verb: only, nothing but
N밖에 + negative = only N, so only 2-3 hours.

**Q42 — D)** 잔
The line is "한 2주? 2주 동안은 하루에 한 2-3시간밖에 못 잔 것 같아." (For about 2 weeks, I think I could only sleep 2 to 3 hours a day.) 자다 (to sleep) in the past modifier form 잔 fits 시간밖에 못. 먹은 (ate) does not fit with hours of sleep.

**Q43 — A)** She recalls something she personally experienced
-더라 reports a firsthand past experience or realization.

**Q44 — B)** 한숨
한숨도 안 자다 = not sleep a wink.

**Q45 — A)** She didn't know who she was and couldn't smile
She says she didn't know who she was and the smile wouldn't come.

**Q46 — B)** Vous voyez ce que je veux dire ?
A recurring phrase checking shared understanding.

**Q47 — D)** Le classique incontournable que tout le monde suit
국룰 = unwritten rule everyone follows.

**Q48 — C)** 뒤집어
뒤집어 주다 = to flip, i.e. tear the place up.

**Q49 — B)** 한테
한테 marks the agent in being scolded by someone.

**Q50 — C)** Elle joue la formalité pour l'effet comique
The mock-formal -ㅂ니다 is comedic.
