# Answer Key

**Q1 — C)** To get one's appetite going / whet one's appetite.
식욕 means appetite, and 돋다 means to be stimulated or to rise. Together, the expression describes appetite being sparked.

**Q2 — C)** The early bird gets drunk quickly.
Hwasa twisted "일찍 일어난 새가 벌레를 잡는다" into "일찍 일어난 새가 빨리 취한다," joking that waking up early leads to getting drunk faster.

**Q3 — B)** 야식.
야식 specifically refers to late-night eating/snacking. 간식 is "snack" generally, without the late-night nuance. 먹방 is an eating broadcast.

**Q4 — C)** 핑계.
The full line is "핑계가 아니야. 정말이야." — It's not an excuse. Really. 변명 (defense/justification) is similar but is not the word used.

**Q5 — B)** S'enivrer / être ivre.
취하다 means to become intoxicated / get drunk. It appears repeatedly throughout Part 2 as Hwasa gets progressively tipsier.

**Q6 — C)** It stimulated her appetite.
She said "멍청이들이랑 얘기하다 보면 식욕이 돋아" — talking with her fans makes her hungry.

**Q7 — B)** To be uninhibited / bold.
거침없다 describes someone who acts without hesitation or restraint. "Reckless" (C) implies negative judgment, which doesn't match the admiring tone Hwasa used about her younger self.

**Q8 — C)** 소름 돋다.
소름 = goosebumps, 돋다 = to rise/appear. She used it when she realized a book passage was actually her own lyrics.

**Q9 — C)** Offrir (un cadeau).
선물하다 means to give something as a gift. Acheter (to buy) and recevoir (to receive) are different actions.

**Q10 — B)** 강퇴.
The full sentence is "이제부터 헛소리 하는 사람들은 모두 강퇴다" — everyone talking nonsense gets kicked out.

**Q11 — C)** A book called "고요한 포옹" (A Quiet Embrace).
Her eldest sister bought it for her at a café in Jeonju that sold books, and Hwasa said she loved the title.

**Q12 — C)** To forget (colloquial).
까먹다 is an informal/colloquial way of saying "to forget." It looks like it could mean "to eat" (먹다 is embedded in it), but the 까- prefix changes the meaning entirely.

**Q13 — B)** Sauvage / à l'état brut.
야생적 means wild or untamed. She used it to describe her younger artistic self — uninhibited and raw, not timid or refined.

**Q14 — C)** 화장 지우다.
지우다 means to erase/remove. 화장 하다 = to put on makeup, 고치다 = to fix/touch up, 바르다 = to apply.

**Q15 — B)** 야생적 — wild / untamed.
She described herself as "굉장히 야생적" and "거침없다" when reflecting on her Pink Panties era, saying she was completely uninhibited and ignorant in a bold way.

**Q16 — C)** 취한다.
The full sentence: "일찍 일어난 날 술 마시면 빨리 취한다" — if you drink on a day you woke up early, you get drunk fast.

**Q17 — B)** A ban or kick from a chatroom.
강퇴 (강제 퇴장) literally means "forced exit." It's commonly used in online/chat contexts for banning or kicking someone out.

**Q18 — C)** Épreuves et tribulations.
고난 = hardship, 시련 = trial/tribulation. Hwasa used this when reflecting philosophically about what the future may hold.

**Q19 — C)** A mukbang (먹방).
The viewer wrote: "라방 처음인데 먹방 보는 것 같아. 배고파져." — It's my first live and it feels like watching a mukbang. I'm getting hungry.

**Q20 — B)** Romantic.
낭만적 directly translates to "romantic." She used it to describe the feeling of reading the book her sister gave her.

**Q21 — C)** 기꺼이.
기꺼이 means gladly or willingly. 겨우 = barely, 갑자기 = suddenly, 솔직히 = honestly — all very different adverbs.

**Q22 — B)** Avoir sommeil / être somnolent(e).
졸리다 specifically means to be sleepy/drowsy. "Être épuisé(e)" (exhausted) is stronger and different. "Être étourdi(e)" (dizzy) is also unrelated.

**Q23 — B)** She discovered the words were actually her own song lyrics.
She was reading and thought the text felt familiar, then realized with goosebumps (소름 돋았어) that it was her own lyrics — "내 맘이야. 가사네. 맞네."

**Q24 — B)** 까먹는.
The full line is "진짜 너무 잘 까먹는 것 같아" — I really seem to forget things too easily. 잊는 (from 잊다) has the same meaning but is not the word used in the transcript.

**Q25 — C)** A price / cost / consequence.
In this context, 대가 means the price or consequence that accompanies happiness. She was reflecting that great happiness often comes with a corresponding cost. Note: 대가 can also mean "great master/expert," but context makes the meaning clear.

**Q26 — C)** Émission culinaire / mukbang.
먹방 (먹는 방송) = eating broadcast. "Cuisine traditionnelle" (traditional cooking) and "recette" (recipe) are unrelated concepts.

**Q27 — C)** Delaying the delivery of something, promising to send it by tomorrow.
She publicly apologized to Dambi, saying "오래 기다리셨는데... 내일까지 꼭 보내드릴게요" — you've waited a long time, I'll definitely send it by tomorrow.

**Q28 — C)** 시.
시 = poem/poetry. 가사 = lyrics, 소설 = novel, 글 = writing/text. A viewer asked if she was reading poetry (시를 읽냐고), and she talked about the book she received.

**Q29 — C)** Looking at nearly identical photos with slightly different vibes back and forth made her feel nauseous, like motion sickness.
She described it as "멀미" (motion sickness) — the photos had the same composition but slightly different vibes, and toggling between them made her feel sick and stressed.

**Q30 — B)** 행복.
The original line from Hwasa's reflection: "거기에 대한 또 대가가 와요" — referring to 행복 (happiness). She had just said "정말 큰 행복이 오잖아" right before, philosophizing that great happiness comes with a price.
