# Answer Key

**Q1 — B)** Discontinued / out of production. 단종 specifically refers to a product being permanently pulled from production, unlike "sold out" (품절) which implies temporary unavailability.

**Q2 — C)** It had been discontinued. She reacted with disbelief, saying 황치즈가 단종이라고?

**Q3 — A)** 포장. 편집 is editing, 반칙 is foul play/cheating, 홍보 is promotion.

**Q4 — B)** Être légèrement salé(e). 짭조름하다 specifically describes a mild, pleasant saltiness — not overwhelmingly salty (A) or spicy (C).

**Q5 — C)** 소개. "I want to introduce/recommend a tasty snack" (소개해주다).

**Q6 — D)** Pleasure in life / life's simple joy. 낙 here means a source of pleasure or comfort, and 삶의 낙 is an idiomatic expression for the small joys that make life worth living.

**Q7 — B)** Coffee. She described coffee as her one and only 삶의 낙 and said she needed to quit it.

**Q8 — C)** Aube / petit matin. 새벽 refers to the hours between roughly 1–5 AM — the pre-dawn period — not nighttime (A) or dusk (B).

**Q9 — A)** 아깝다. It conveys that something valuable is being wasted or that it's a pity to lose it. 한심하다 means pathetic, 단호하다 means firm/decisive.

**Q10 — C)** Nutty / savory / toasty. 고소하다 is a distinctly Korean flavor descriptor for the warm, roasted, nutty taste found in sesame, toasted grains, or certain cheeses.

**Q11 — B)** Être adorable / charmant(e). 사랑스럽다 means lovable or adorable. Être amoureux (A) means to be in love, which is a different meaning entirely.

**Q12 — C)** She was admiring the packaging of a snack she was showing to fans during the live, praising how lovely it looked even before opening it.

**Q13 — B)** 디카페인. She said she doesn't really like decaf (디카페인을 별로 안 좋아한단 말이야).

**Q14 — C)** Sense of unfamiliarity / feeling of disconnect. Not nausea (B) or nostalgia (A). 이질감 describes something feeling "off" or foreign.

**Q15 — B)** 갈망. While 욕심 (A) can mean desire, 갈망 carries a deeper, more intense sense of yearning or craving.

**Q16 — C)** The acidity in coffee. 산미 (酸味) literally means "sour/acid taste" and is a standard term used in Korean coffee culture to describe a coffee's acidity profile.

**Q17 — C)** Être ferme / catégorique. 단호하다 describes being resolute and firm, not cruel (D) or hesitant (B).

**Q18 — B)** Soft/squishy and chewy/springy. These are onomatopoeic texture words: 말랑말랑 = soft and yielding, 쫀득쫀득 = pleasantly chewy with elasticity.

**Q19 — C)** 은은. "은은하게 달아" = "It's subtly sweet." 은은하다 describes something gentle and understated.

**Q20 — B)** Pizza bread is so good it's basically unfair/cheating. 반칙 literally means "foul" (as in sports), but colloquially it's used to say something is unfairly good or irresistible.

**Q21 — C)** Jet lag / time difference. 시차 (時差) combines 시 (time) and 차 (difference).

**Q22 — C)** 딱딱하다. It means hard or stiff — the opposite of 말랑말랑하다 (soft/squishy).

**Q23 — B)** She kept falling asleep at dawn despite trying to reset her body clock (시차를 돌리려고). She was struggling with a reversed sleep schedule.

**Q24 — B)** To show off / to make a big deal of doing something. 생색을 내다 implies taking exaggerated credit for a favor or good deed.

**Q25 — C)** Être abondant / avoir en abondance. 풍족하다 means having plenty, being well-supplied. It's about abundance, not just wealth (D) or satisfaction (B).

**Q26 — B)** She was reflecting on a time in the past when having plenty of food wasn't easy, which created a lasting 갈망 (craving/longing) that still stays with her.

**Q27 — B)** 최애. A compound of 최 (最, most) + 애 (愛, love) — literally "most loved," used as slang for one's absolute favorite.

**Q28 — C)** 나눠줬어. "Bought a few boxes and shared them with friends again" (나눠주다 = to share/distribute).

**Q29 — C)** Arrêter / couper. 끊다 means to cut off or quit. Commencer (A) means to start — the opposite.

**Q30 — B)** She was describing how firmly or decisively she herself had spoken about something. 단호하게 말하다 = to speak in a firm, no-nonsense manner.
