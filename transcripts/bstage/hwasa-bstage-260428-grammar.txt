=== [00:27] 그래서 어제 급하게 나가가지고 ===

--- word ---
term: 그래서
root: 그렇다 (to be so)
breakdown: 그래 + 서
pos: conjunction
definition: so; therefore
notes: A very common connective meaning 'so' or 'therefore.' Derived from 그렇다 + 아서. Used constantly in spoken Korean to link cause and effect.

--- word ---
term: 어제
root: 어제 (yesterday)
breakdown: 어제
pos: adverb
definition: yesterday
notes: Basic time adverb. No conjugation needed.

--- word ---
term: 급하게
root: 급하다 (to be urgent/hurried)
breakdown: 급하 + 게
pos: adverb (derived from adjective)
definition: urgently; in a hurry
notes: 급하다 is a Sino-Korean adjective (急). The ~게 ending converts an adjective into an adverb. Pattern: ADJ-게 = 'in a [ADJ] way.'

--- word ---
term: 나가가지고
root: 나가다 (to go out)
breakdown: 나가 + 가지고
pos: verb (connective form)
definition: went out and (then)…
notes: 나가다 = 나 (out) + 가다 (to go). The ending ~아/어 가지고 is a colloquial connective equivalent to ~아/어서 (because/and then). Very common in casual speech. Often shortened to ~갖고 in fast speech.

--- grammar ---
pattern: ADJ-게
meaning: Converts a descriptive verb (adjective) into an adverb — 'in a [ADJ] manner'
example: 급하게
level: beginner

--- grammar ---
pattern: V-아/어 가지고
meaning: Casual connective meaning 'and then' or 'because (I did V).' Colloquial equivalent of V-아/어서.
example: 나가가지고
level: intermediate

meaning: Vocative particle for calling someone by name. 아 after consonant, 야 after vowel.

=== [00:36] 할머니들한테 인사해 ===

--- word ---
term: 할머니들
root: 할머니 (grandmother)
breakdown: 할머니 + 들
pos: noun + plural particle
definition: grandmothers; the grandmothers
notes: 들 is the plural marker. Hwasa is referring to the viewers/fans affectionately, or to actual elderly people present. The use of 할머니 could also be playful depending on context.

--- word ---
term: 한테
root: 한테 (to; toward a person)
breakdown: 한테
pos: particle
definition: to (a person)
notes: Casual equivalent of 에게. Used to indicate the recipient of an action. Only used for animate beings (people, animals). For places/things, use 에.

--- word ---
term: 인사해
root: 인사하다 (to greet)
breakdown: 인사 + 하 + 해
pos: verb (imperative/casual)
definition: say hello; greet (them)
notes: 인사 (人事) is a Sino-Korean noun meaning 'greeting.' 인사하다 = to greet. 인사해 is the casual imperative (반말) form — giving a friendly command. Pattern: NOUN + 하다 verb.

--- grammar ---
pattern: N-들
meaning: Plural marker attached to nouns
example: 할머니들
level: beginner

--- grammar ---
pattern: N-한테
meaning: Casual particle meaning 'to (a person).' Equivalent of 에게.
example: 할머니들한테
level: beginner

--- grammar ---
pattern: V-해 (imperative)
meaning: Casual command form of 하다 verbs. 반말 register.
example: 인사해
level: beginner

=== [00:47] 아직 집에 도착을 못했어? ===

--- word ---
term: 아직
root: 아직 (still; yet)
breakdown: 아직
pos: adverb
definition: yet; still
notes: In questions, 아직 often translates to 'yet.' In statements, it means 'still.' Here in a question: 'haven't you [yet]…?'

--- word ---
term: 집에
root: 집 (house/home)
breakdown: 집 + 에
pos: noun + particle
definition: at home; to home
notes: 에 is the location/destination particle. With 도착 (arrival), 집에 means 'at home' (the destination of arrival).

--- word ---
term: 도착을
root: 도착 (arrival)
breakdown: 도착 + 을
pos: noun + object particle
definition: arrival (as object)
notes: 도착 (到着) is a Sino-Korean noun. 을 marks it as the object. In Korean, you can say 도착을 하다 ('do arrival') — splitting the noun-하다 verb and inserting the object particle for emphasis or to insert 못.

--- word ---
term: 못했어
root: 하다 (to do)
breakdown: 못 + 했어
pos: adverb + verb (past casual)
definition: couldn't do; wasn't able to
notes: 못 is a negation adverb meaning 'cannot.' 했어 is past tense casual of 하다. Together 못했어 = 'couldn't do.' The full construction 도착을 못했어 = 'couldn't arrive' = 'haven't arrived.'

--- grammar ---
pattern: 못 + V
meaning: Negation of ability — 'cannot V' or 'failed to V'
example: 못했어
level: beginner

--- grammar ---
pattern: N-을/를 못하다
meaning: 'Cannot do N' — splitting a NOUN+하다 verb to insert 못 for negation
example: 도착을 못했어
level: intermediate

--- grammar ---
pattern: V-았/었어?
meaning: Past tense casual question form (반말)
example: 못했어?
level: beginner
