# Answer Key

**Q1 — C)** Tear(s). 눈물 literally combines 눈 (eye) + 물 (water). A) 눈 alone can mean snow, but 눈물 specifically means tears.

**Q2 — B)** Fans had prepared an album project for her. She called them 멍청이들 (idiots) affectionately, overcome with emotion that they would do something like that.

**Q3 — C)** 한정판. 품절 means "sold out," 매진 also means "sold out," and 정규 means "regular/full-length."

**Q4 — B)** S'estomper / devenir flou. 희미해지다 means to fade or become faint/blurry, not to disappear completely (A) or to glow (C).

**Q5 — C)** 운동. She said she's been working out diligently lately (운동을 하고 있는데).

**Q6 — C)** Burden / pressure. 부담감 is the feeling of being burdened or pressured, not excitement or gratitude.

**Q7 — B)** 한심하다. It specifically means pathetic or pitiful. 어렵다 means difficult, 희미해지다 means to fade, and 행복하다 means happy.

**Q8 — B)** A particular item/product that had been sold out for 8 years. She was reacting with surprise to learning something had been 매진 (sold out) that long.

**Q9 — B)** Émotion / sentiment. Not to be confused with passion/obsession (A) or impression/avis (D), which carry different nuances.

**Q10 — B)** 눈물. "Tears are coming out because of these idiots" — an affectionate, emotional reaction toward fans.

**Q11 — C)** 충고. 비유 means metaphor, 홍보 means promotion, and 욕심 means ambition/desire.

**Q12 — B)** She was describing how once she picks up a hobby, she becomes deeply invested and wants to do it properly — the 욕심 (ambition/desire) kicks in.

**Q13 — B)** To be swollen / puffy. 퉁퉁 is an adverb emphasizing the degree of swelling. It doesn't mean blushing (C) or weight gain (A).

**Q14 — C)** 비유. A metaphor or analogy. 충고 is advice, 감정 is emotion, 편집 is editing.

**Q15 — C)** Épuisé / en rupture de stock. 품절 means sold out / out of stock. "En promotion" (B) would mean on sale, which is the opposite situation.

**Q16 — B)** She was expressing playful disbelief or surprise at the idea of her comeback, not making a formal announcement.

**Q17 — C)** 편집. In context, she was talking about video/album editing being almost finished.

**Q18 — C)** 식단. She said she's currently on a diet/meal plan (식단을 하고 있지만).

**Q19 — C)** A full-length / regular album. 정규 앨범 is the standard Korean music industry term for a full studio album, as opposed to a mini-album or single.

**Q20 — B)** She was playfully exaggerating — saying she'd make about 10 full albums to humorously express her enthusiasm. Not a literal production commitment.

**Q21 — C)** Passe-temps / loisir. 취미 means hobby/pastime. Habitude (B) means habit, which is subtly different — a hobby is chosen for enjoyment, a habit is a repeated behavior.

**Q22 — C)** 행복하다. 맛있다 means delicious, 한심하다 means pathetic, 어렵다 means difficult.

**Q23 — B)** The editing is almost done. 거의 다 means "almost all / nearly finished."

**Q24 — B)** Publicity / promotion. 홍보 specifically refers to PR/promotional activities, not rehearsal or sponsorship.

**Q25 — D)** Être délicieux / bon. While C) "être savoureux" is close, the standard and most accurate French equivalent taught for 맛있다 is "être délicieux / bon."

**Q26 — B)** She was suggesting that future albums should be produced as limited editions (한정판으로 찍다).

**Q27 — D)** 욕심. It can mean greed, desire, or ambition depending on context. 부담감 is pressure, 감정 is emotion, 충고 is advice.

**Q28 — B)** 행복. "너무 행복한 삶이다" = "It's such a happy life." Note the adjective form 행복한 modifying 삶.

**Q29 — C)** Être difficile. While D) "compliqué(e) et déroutant(e)" is in the same semantic field, the direct and standard translation of 어렵다 is simply "être difficile."

**Q30 — B)** She was commenting on being cold right as she started the live broadcast. There was no mention of an ice bucket challenge or weather report.
