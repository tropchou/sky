# Answer Key

**Q1 — C)** Give in to her sleepiness
굴복하다 means to give in or submit, and here she is fighting sleep ("If I sleep now, I lose"). Nothing in the lines mentions food or ending the stream.

**Q2 — D)** 더라고
"피부관리 되는 그 유산균이 있더라고." means "Apparently there are probiotics that are good for your skin." -더라고 reports something she has found or observed. The other endings don't fit that meaning.

**Q3 — B)** It echoes what someone else said, here with a note of disbelief ("Who says I'm sleepy?")
-ㄴ대/-는대 repeats or questions something someone else has said. She is pushing back on chat comments saying she looks sleepy.

**Q4 — B)** Mais qu'est-ce que vous racontez ?
소리 literally means "sound", but 무슨 소리야? is an idiom for "What nonsense are you saying?". She is rejecting the idea that she is sleepy.

**Q5 — D)** Nobody can leave, so everyone should stay put and keep fighting sleep
It is a mock-strict order: nobody leaves the room, everyone stays and holds on. This follows "모두 참아" and "가만히 있어라".

**Q6 — A)** A blunt plain-form command (-어라), used jokingly with the chat
-어라/-아라 is a direct imperative, normally used toward someone younger or very close. Here it is teasing, in the same joking tone as "Nobody leaves this room".

**Q7 — C)** It isn't contagion; everyone is probably just really sleepy
She says 전염이 아니라, 진짜 졸린 걸 거야 다들: it's not contagion, you're all probably just sleepy. She turns the accusation back on the viewers.

**Q8 — B)** 를
"난 렌즈를 못 껴" means "I can't wear contact lenses." 렌즈 is the object of 끼다, so it takes the object particle 를. The others don't fit this function.

**Q9 — D)** It is playful role-play: formal thanks, calling the commenter "Mother" as a joke
The story bible notes 어머니 is a teasing address to a commenter. The formal 감사합니다 adds comedy because the thanks is only mock-respectful.

**Q10 — A)** To squeeze (a pimple)
Here 짜다 means to squeeze or press something out, like a pimple. 짜다 can also mean "salty", but that makes no sense for a pimple.

**Q11 — B)** That it is not a pimple
She said 그건 여드름이 아닙니다: "That's not a pimple." Other people keep mistaking the old eyebrow mole for acne.

**Q12 — B)** A feeling of comfort or ease
편안하다 means to be comfortable or at ease, and -ㅁ makes it a noun. Resting her hand on her chin feels comfortable to her.

**Q13 — C)** It is in a spot that is clearly visible when she wears a tank top
She says it is a spot that shows right away in a 나시 (sleeveless top). It is a different mole from the eyebrow one.

**Q14 — D)** Comment dire ?
뭐라 그래야지 is a filler used while searching for words, like "how should I put it?". It is the same as 뭐라 그래야 되지 elsewhere in the stream.

**Q15 — A)** She is relaying something she heard ("they say it's the last")
-(이)래 is the contraction of -(이)라고 해, so it is hearsay: "They say that if you have a mole on your left hand, this life is your last."

**Q16 — A)** 나 보네
"왼손에 점 있는 사람들이 꽤 있나 보네." means "Apparently quite a few people have a mole on their left hand." -나 보다 guesses from what she observes, here the chat's comments.

**Q17 — B)** Polite and honorific (-(으)시나요), as a respectful question; she is reading a fan's message aloud
-(으)시- plus -나요 is a courteous, honorific question. She is quoting a viewer's question, so it is not her usual casual speech.

**Q18 — C)** It appeals to shared understanding: "it could be, right? You know that"
-ㄹ 수도 있다 means "might be", and -잖아 adds "as you know / right?". She suggests that in a parallel universe she might not even be human.

**Q19 — B)** 에서
"나는 평행우주에서 무뚝뚝한 고구마집이야" means "In the parallel universe, I'm a Mutukduk sweet potato chip." 에서 marks the place or setting. The others don't fit.

**Q20 — A)** A Mutukduk sweet potato chip
She says she's a Mutukduk sweet potato chip (고구마집 = 고구마칩). She then jokes that if someone eats her, that's the end.

**Q21 — C)** 환생
"환생도 못하고" means "without even being able to be reborn." 환생 is reincarnation. She jokes she'd be stuck as a chip, unable to be reborn.

**Q22 — D)** Purpose or result: "so that I can be reborn"
After "차라리 먹어줘" (just eat me), -게 gives the aim: so that I can be reborn.

**Q23 — B)** 잔인한
"너 굉장히 잔인한 아이구나" means "You're a really cruel one." She says this after being told to freeze-dry her (동결건조). The other adjectives don't fit that reaction.

**Q24 — B)** To go radio silent or disappear without contact
잠수 타다 literally means to dive, but it is slang for vanishing without contact. She promises she won't disappear on fans this time.

**Q25 — B)** She has a huge pile of things to do right now
산더미 (a mountain) is used figuratively for a heap of tasks. It supports her promise not to go radio silent.

**Q26 — D)** Somehow, one way or another
어떻게든 (어떻게 + -든) means "by whatever means". She says that if needed she'll cry, but she'll manage somehow.

**Q27 — B)** One's right mind; she was not fully in her senses
제정신 means sound mind. 제정신이 아니다 means to be out of it, as she was after no sleep at the pre-recording.

**Q28 — A)** "If you're going to be like this (anyway)", a slightly critical or puzzled tone
-(으)ㄹ 거면 means "if that's how it's going to be". She imagines dazed fans looking like they wonder why they came at all.

**Q29 — C)** Being dazed or zoned out
넋이 나가다 literally means the spirit has left, so it means dazed or spaced out. She is describing fans at the pre-dawn recording.

**Q30 — A)** 에
"왕관도 막 여기 목에 내려와 있고" means "Their crowns had slipped down around their necks." 에 marks the place they landed. The others don't fit with 내려와 있다.

**Q31 — A)** Their paper crowns had slid down around their necks
She describes dazed fans whose crowns had slipped down to their necks. This is part of the funny picture of the pre-dawn recording.

**Q32 — C)** 겠다고
"하여튼 그 상태에서 응원을 하겠다고 왔잖아" means "Anyway, in that state, they still came to cheer me on." -겠다고 states an intention: they came saying they would cheer.

**Q33 — C)** A guess based on observation: "they must be..."
-ㄴ가 보다 guesses from what she has seen, here that the fans go quiet in front of the camera. -긴 하다 adds "it seems so, indeed".

**Q34 — C)** The fans' cheering chants suddenly get very loud
At rehearsal the fans are quiet, but when the staff say recording begins, the chants become huge. She calls that being a pro.

**Q35 — B)** A tentative thought: "I wonder if / I think maybe this is..."
-(으)ㄹ까 싶다 expresses a soft, tentative opinion. She thinks that the cheering switch is exactly what a pro is.

**Q36 — A)** To covet or want to have
탐내다 means to covet or desire something. She asks who coveted the crown, but not that anyone stole it.

**Q37 — D)** 자마자
"도착하자마자" means "as soon as I arrived". -자마자 links two actions that happen right after one another; she arrived and then passed out.

**Q38 — D)** To feel regret or wistfulness about something
아쉽다 describes the feeling that something fell short or you wish it had gone differently. She explains her regrets about the Maria era.

**Q39 — D)** After continuing to do something, a result or realization follows
-다 보니(까) means "as I kept doing X, Y came about". She kept going through injections and activities, and her body ended up bigger.

**Q40 — A)** She has seen the music video but never watched the music show stages
She says 뮤비는 봤는데 음악방송 한 걸 한 번도 안 봤어. She dislikes how she looked then, with back pain and a bloated body.

**Q41 — A)** 행복한
"So Cute is also, in a way, a really happy song, you know." She makes the point that purely happy songs can be hard to sing on a tired day.

**Q42 — D)** Sometimes a bit painful
고통스럽다 means painful. She says singing a happy song while feeling low is sometimes a bit painful.

**Q43 — D)** 몰입
"뭔가 그냥 바로 몰입이 되는 느낌이야" means "I feel like I get immersed immediately." This is how she describes dark songs like Twit and Maria.

**Q44 — D)** Purely happy songs are actually harder for her
오히려 means "on the contrary". Purely happy songs are actually harder than dark ones, which she can sink into right away.

**Q45 — A)** 잖아
"그러다가 좀 소큐트가 나오면 기분이 좋아지잖아" means "and then when So Cute comes on, you'll feel better, right?" -잖아 appeals to shared experience.

**Q46 — C)** 의견
"막 다 각자의 이런 의견들이 있잖아" means "everybody has their own opinions, right?" 의견 means opinion, in the context of politics.

**Q47 — C)** 한테
"그래서 나는 어렸을 때 그랬어 아빠한테" means "when I was little, I said that to my dad." 한테 marks the person spoken to.

**Q48 — B)** 건강한
"그것도 건강한 사회가 아니라고" means "that wouldn't be a healthy society either." Her father says a society running on one opinion is not healthy.

**Q49 — A)** En douce, discrètement
은근슬쩍 means subtly, on the sly. She teases a fan for sneaking the word "father-in-law" into the comment.

**Q50 — C)** 에
"트위터에 올려" means "Post it on Twitter." 에 marks the destination where it is posted. The other particles don't fit.
