# Answer Key


# Answer Key

---

**Q1 — b**
신기하다 means to be fascinating/amazing/incredible.

---

**Q2 — b**
She said she failed to adjust to the time difference again (실패한 거야).

---

**Q3 — c**
She was describing how a place she visited on her trip felt like stepping into a storybook.

---

**Q4 — c**
핑계 means excuse/pretext. 용서 = forgiveness, 예매 = advance booking, 체력 = stamina.

---

**Q5 — b**
행복하다 = être heureux/heureuse (to be happy).

---

**Q6 — b**
귀찮다 expresses that something is bothersome or that you can't be bothered to do it.

---

**Q7 — b**
She was explaining that McDonald's breakfast delivery doesn't service her area.

---

**Q8 — c**
"행복하려고 사는 거 아니겠어?" = "Aren't we living to be happy?" 사는 comes from 살다 (to live), not 사다 (to buy).

---

**Q9 — d**
체력 means stamina/physical strength. 운동 = exercise, 시차 = time difference, 배달 = delivery.

---

**Q10 — b**
예매 = réservation (de billets) — advance ticket booking/reservation.

---

**Q11 — b**
힘들다 means something is exhausting, difficult, or tough.

---

**Q12 — c**
She ate goulash during her European trip and described it as really delicious.

---

**Q13 — b**
떨어졌네요 (dropped/declined) — her stamina had decreased.

---

**Q14 — a**
시차 적응 = adjusting to the time difference / jet lag adjustment.

---

**Q15 — c**
웃기다 = être drôle / être marrant(e) (to be funny).

---

**Q16 — b**
처음 here means "first time" — it was everyone's first time going.

---

**Q17 — b**
She's saying she gets excited whenever a reason/excuse to do something arises — it's a positive feeling, not guilt.

---

**Q18 — d**
맛있었어 — the goulash was really delicious.

---

**Q19 — b**
처음 = première fois / pour la première fois.

---

**Q20 — c**
배달 = delivery. 동화책 = fairy tale book, 감자튀김 = fries, 예매 = booking.

---

**Q21 — b**
졸리다 specifically means feeling sleepy/drowsy, not just generally tired (which would be 피곤하다).

---

**Q22 — c**
She was explaining that because she hadn't exercised much, her stamina had dropped — connecting 운동 to 체력.

---

**Q23 — d**
자다 = dormir (to sleep).

---

**Q24 — b**
도전 = challenge/attempt. It was her first attempt at a morning live broadcast.

---

**Q25 — b**
싫어 expresses dislike or not wanting something.

---

**Q26 — a**
She was attempting a morning live broadcast for the very first time (최초 = first ever, 도전 = attempt/challenge).

---

**Q27 — b**
동화책 = fairy tale book (동화 = fairy tale + 책 = book).

---

**Q28 — c**
용서 = pardon (forgiveness).

---

**Q29 — c**
She said she went to sleep at 7 (7시에 잤어).

---

**Q30 — b**
"용서가 돼" implies that something can be forgiven or excused — "I can let it slide."
