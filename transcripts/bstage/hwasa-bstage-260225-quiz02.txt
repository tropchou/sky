# Answer Key

---

**Q1 — b**
뒹굴뒹굴 is a mimetic word describing rolling around lazily or lounging about.

---

**Q2 — c**
취소하다 means to cancel. 견디다 = to endure, 사라지다 = to disappear, 혼내다 = to scold.

---

**Q3 — b**
"똥꼬가 너무 보고싶은거야" — She was saying she missed her pet 똥꼬 so much.

---

**Q4 — b**
조퇴 means leaving early (from school or work). She was playfully announcing she'd leave the broadcast early.

---

**Q5 — c**
눈물 = larmes (tears). Bâillement = yawn, lunettes = glasses, vue = vision.

---

**Q6 — b**
꿀밤 is a forehead flick — a playful physical punishment, often between friends or family. She jokingly said she gave 500 of them.

---

**Q7 — d**
시력 = eyesight/vision. 노화 = aging, 안경 = glasses, 눈물 = tears.

---

**Q8 — b**
존경하다 = respecter / admirer (to respect/admire).

---

**Q9 — b**
She envied people who wore glasses and deliberately tried to worsen her eyesight so she could get a pair.

---

**Q10 — c**
"시력이 안 좋아진 것 같아" — She felt her eyesight had gotten worse (좋아진 from 좋아지다, to get better, negated by 안).

---

**Q11 — b**
부럽다 means to be jealous or envious of something.

---

**Q12 — b**
반찬투정 means being fussy about side dishes. She was admitting she was a picky eater as a child.

---

**Q13 — b**
공복 = estomac vide / à jeun (empty stomach / fasting).

---

**Q14 — b**
만만하게 보다 means to look down on or underestimate someone. 보고 싶다 = to miss someone, which is very different despite both containing 보다.

---

**Q15 — c**
She was telling a story about her mother disappearing — the 셨 honorific form confirms she was referring to an elder.

---

**Q16 — c**
노화 means aging. It came up when she was joking about why her eyes were tearing up.

---

**Q17 — b**
"공복을 런닝해야 되는데" — She needed to go running on an empty stomach.

---

**Q18 — c**
폭설 = fortes chutes de neige / tempête de neige (heavy snowfall / snowstorm).

---

**Q19 — c**
개종 means religious conversion. She was telling a story about her mother's spiritual journey.

---

**Q20 — b**
견디다 means to endure or bear. 붓다 = to swell, 혼내다 = to scold, 취소하다 = to cancel.

---

**Q21 — b**
붓다 means to swell. She was describing physical swelling, likely of her face.

---

**Q22 — c**
혼내다 = gronder / réprimander (to scold / to reprimand).

---

**Q23 — c**
로망 (from the French "roman") is used in Korean to mean a dream or romantic ideal — here, a childhood dream.

---

**Q24 — b**
"우리 엄마를 제일 존경한답니다" — She said she respects her mom the most.

---

**Q25 — c**
안경 = lunettes (glasses). Larmes = tears, acuité visuelle = visual acuity, bâillement = yawn.

---

**Q26 — c**
충격적 means shocking or overwhelming. She was describing how startling her mom's story was.

---

**Q27 — b**
"혼내진 않아" means "I didn't get scolded" — the -진 않다 construction softens the negation.

---

**Q28 — b**
하품 = yawn. 눈물 = tears, 공복 = empty stomach, 폭설 = heavy snowfall.

---

**Q29 — a**
A fan in the chat mentioned heavy snowfall in Daegu, and Hwasa reacted with surprise.

---

**Q30 — b**
만만하게 보다 means to underestimate or look down on someone. She was warning not to take someone lightly.
