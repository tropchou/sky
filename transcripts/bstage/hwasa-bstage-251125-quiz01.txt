# Answer Key

**Q1 — B)** To be overwhelmed / overcome with emotion.
벅차다 expresses being so full of emotion that it's hard to contain — not exhaustion or frustration.

**Q2 — C)** A hot spring (온천).
She mentioned going to an onsen/hot spring during her trip and finding it very relaxing.

**Q3 — A)** 유통기한.
This is the standard Korean term for "expiration date" or "shelf life." 소비기한 and 유효기간 are related but are not the term used in the transcript.

**Q4 — B)** 뿌얀.
She commented early in the broadcast that the screen looked blurry/hazy (뿌얀).

**Q5 — C)** Avoir faim.
배고프다 = to be hungry. "Être rassasié(e)" would be 배부르다 (the opposite).

**Q6 — B)** Hiding things / keeping secrets out of habit.
She reflected that hiding things (숨기다) had become second nature (버릇), even when it wasn't a comeback situation.

**Q7 — C)** To be bothersome / can't be bothered.
귀찮다 is about something feeling like a hassle. It looks similar to 귀엽다 (cute) but has a completely different meaning.

**Q8 — B)** 쟁여두다.
This colloquial verb means to stock up or hoard supplies. 숨기다 means to hide, which is different.

**Q9 — C)** Ravioli / boulette (dumpling).
만두 are Korean dumplings, most accurately translated in French as ravioli or boulette.

**Q10 — B)** 배달.
The full sentence is "배달 음식을 차라리 시키고 싶은데" — she'd rather order delivery food.

**Q11 — C)** 행복하다.
She said "너무 행복해" when describing how she felt. While she also expressed gratitude (감사하다) and being overwhelmed (벅차다), 행복하다 was the word tied to her general state.

**Q12 — C)** To be sudden / unexpected.
갑작스럽다 describes something that happens abruptly or without warning.

**Q13 — B)** Cacher / dissimuler.
숨기다 means to hide or conceal. Chercher (to search) and montrer (to show) are the opposite actions.

**Q14 — D)** 감사하다.
감사하다 specifically means to be grateful/thankful. 벅차다 is about being overwhelmed, which is related but distinct.

**Q15 — B)** Select photos (사진 셀렉).
She mentioned needing to do a photo selection (셀렉) that day.

**Q16 — B)** 쟁여둬.
The original line was "역시 쟁여둬야 돼" — you really have to stock up. The other options (숨겨둬 = hide away, 감춰둬 = conceal, 챙겨둬 = pack/take care of) are plausible but incorrect.

**Q17 — B)** Something that is blurry / hazy.
뿌옇다 describes a lack of visual clarity — like fog or a smudged screen.

**Q18 — B)** Émotif / sensible.
감성적 means emotional or sentimental in a reflective, feeling-oriented sense.

**Q19 — C)** Peeling / preparing them is too bothersome.
She used 귀찮다 about the fuss involved with shrimp dumplings (꽃기가 너무 귀찮아), referring to how annoying they are to deal with.

**Q20 — C)** Refrigerator.
냉장고 = refrigerator. A freezer would be 냉동고.

**Q21 — A)** 보고 싶다.
This is the standard Korean expression for missing someone. 만나고 싶다 (want to meet) is close but lacks the emotional nuance of longing.

**Q22 — C)** Voyage.
여행 = voyage (trip/travel). Promenade implies a casual walk, and excursion suggests a short outing — neither captures the full meaning.

**Q23 — B)** 편.
The sentence is "너무 편해서" — because it was so comfortable. This referred to how relaxed she felt.

**Q24 — B)** She said it wasn't a comeback, so there was no need to hide anything.
She remarked "이건 뭐 컴백도 아닌데 숨길 필요가 뭐 있어," reflecting on her habit of being secretive even when it's unnecessary.

**Q25 — B)** 배달.
배달 = delivery. 배부르다 (to be full) looks visually similar but is unrelated.

**Q26 — C)** Être heureux/heureuse.
행복하다 = to be happy. "Être reconnaissant(e)" corresponds to 감사하다.

**Q27 — B)** A habit of rummaging through the fridge when wearing comfortable clothes at home.
She described how wearing loungewear (용통) at home leads to raiding the fridge (냉장고 뒤져).

**Q28 — D)** 운동.
운동 = exercise/workout. She mentioned having to go work out the next day.

**Q29 — B)** 유통기한.
The original line is "유통기한이 얼마 안 남았어" — the expiration date is coming up soon. This matches the exact term from the transcript.

**Q30 — B)** 감성적.
She described herself as 감성적 (emotional/sentimental) to explain why she gets easily moved. 예민한 (sensitive) has a more negative/irritable connotation.
