# Answer Key

**Q1 — B)** To be hot / trendy / popular. 핫하다 is a Konglish adaptation of "hot" used to describe something trendy. It does not refer to temperature or spiciness in this context.

**Q2 — C)** Rare and precious — not easily available. 귀하다 describes something valued because of its scarcity. She was reflecting on how certain foods weren't common or easy to come by when she was young.

**Q3 — B)** 문방구. A 문방구 is the classic Korean neighborhood stationery/school supply shop, often also selling snacks and small toys. 편의점 is a convenience store, 서점 is a bookstore.

**Q4 — B)** Brochette de cœur (de poulet). 염통 specifically refers to the heart organ (usually chicken), and 꼬치 means skewer.

**Q5 — A)** 필수. "Chicken hearts are a must/essential." She stated it as non-negotiable.

**Q6 — C)** Likes and dislikes / divisive in opinion. 호불호 combines 호 (好, like) and 불호 (不好, dislike) — it describes something that polarizes opinions.

**Q7 — B)** She thought 피순대 (blood sausage) would be a divisive food. 호불호가 갈리다 means opinions are split — some people love it, others can't stand it.

**Q8 — C)** Vermicelles de patate douce. 당면 is made from sweet potato starch. Rice noodles (A) would be 쌀국수, and buckwheat noodles (B) would be 메밀국수.

**Q9 — A)** 충격적. It means shocking or startling. Be careful not to confuse 충격 (shock) with 충고 (advice) — similar first syllable, very different meanings.

**Q10 — B)** She was afraid of 초장 as a child. She said 어렸을 땐 난 초장을 무서워했거든 — an unexpected and humorous admission.

**Q11 — B)** Être croustillant / croquant. 바삭하다 specifically describes that satisfying crispy/crunchy texture. 촉촉하다 (moist) is its opposite.

**Q12 — C)** To be moist / soft / dewy. 촉촉하다 describes a pleasant moistness or softness, the opposite of dry or crispy.

**Q13 — B)** She prefers 바삭 (crispy), but mentioned that she sometimes 양보 (yields/gives up) the crispy parts to others.

**Q14 — B)** 양보하다. It means to concede or yield something to another person. 나눠주다 means to share/distribute, 끊다 means to quit, 발라 먹다 means to debone and eat.

**Q15 — C)** 발라. "I'm really good at picking the flesh off cutlassfish" (발라 먹다).

**Q16 — C)** Picking the flesh off bones or deboning fish and eating it. 발라 먹다 is specifically used for the skill of cleanly separating edible meat from bones, especially fish.

**Q17 — B)** Poisson-sabre braisé. 갈치 is cutlassfish/hairtail (a long, silver, blade-shaped fish), and 조림 means braised/simmered.

**Q18 — B)** To be oily / greasy / fatty. 기름지다 describes food with a high fat/oil content.

**Q19 — A)** She doesn't enjoy fatty/oily types of raw fish (회). She was being specific — not rejecting all sashimi, just the fattier varieties.

**Q20 — C)** 철이 없다. Literally "to not have 철 (season/maturity)," meaning to be immature or not yet sensible. 한심하다 means pathetic, 단호하다 means firm, 서운하다 means feeling let down.

**Q21 — B)** Side dish / something on the side. 곁다리 implies something supplementary or secondary, not the main attraction.

**Q22 — C)** 서운. "If shrimp tempura is missing, I feel let down" (서운하다).

**Q23 — B)** If shrimp tempura is missing, she feels let down — it's essential. 서운하다 here conveys that gentle sense of disappointment, as if something important has been left out.

**Q24 — B)** Récit / narration / histoire (épique). 서사 refers to a narrative or saga — a grand, ongoing story. Not to be confused with scénario (D), which is more technical/screenwriting-specific.

**Q25 — C)** A rare item (gaming/collectible slang). 레어템 is a portmanteau of "rare" + 아이템 (item), borrowed from gaming culture but used broadly to describe anything rare and special.

**Q26 — B)** Conceit / arrogance / vanity. 자만 carries a negative connotation — it's excessive pride beyond healthy self-confidence (A).

**Q27 — C)** She was clarifying that her words shouldn't be taken as arrogance — she was being genuine and sincere, not boastful. The phrase 자만을 한다는 게 아니고 is a preemptive disclaimer.

**Q28 — C)** Être précieux / cher (au cœur). 소중하다 describes something deeply cherished and dear to one's heart, more emotionally weighted than simply "important" (A) or "useful" (D).

**Q29 — B)** The beginning of a new chapter in the ongoing story/narrative between her and her fans. 서사 here refers to the shared history and continuing saga of their relationship.

**Q30 — B)** 직화. 직 (直, direct) + 화 (火, fire) = direct-flame grilling. 바삭 describes texture (crispy), 조림 means braising, and 꼬치 means skewer.
